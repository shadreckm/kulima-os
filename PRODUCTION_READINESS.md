# Kulima OS Production Readiness

## Purpose

This document describes how Kulima OS evaluates production readiness for energy infrastructure planning. Production readiness is not only about deployment architecture; it is also about the system's ability to produce institution-grade, auditable guidance while preserving Zero-PII and coordination-first invariants.

## Production Readiness Criteria

Kulima OS evaluates production readiness along four core dimensions:

- **Technical Readiness**: Are demand signals validated, load estimates conservative, and infrastructure requirements clear?
- **Financial Readiness**: Is there a rough order of magnitude capex estimate, a tariff/regulatory path, and evidence that costs can be recovered responsibly?
- **Institutional Readiness**: Has utility engagement, regulatory compliance, finance partner outreach, and governance framework planning been identified?
- **Community Readiness**: Has stakeholder consultation, service connection planning, tariff awareness, and safe-use training been defined?

## Readiness Artifacts

The primary production-readiness artifact is the Demand-Signal Prospectus generated by `core/prospectus/prospectus_generator.py`.

Key output sections include:

- `deployment_readiness`
  - infrastructure sizing and protection requirements
  - civil works and service connection assumptions
  - estimated capex and contingency guidance
  - phased implementation timeline
  - stakeholder engagement status
  - regulatory and compliance checklist
  - readiness assessment labels for technical, financial, institutional, community, and overall readiness
  - next steps for deployment

- `planning_reserve`
  - explicit planning reserve information required by KULIMA OS
  - ensures capacity design preserves communal productive assets and essential services

- `risk_governance`
  - demand uncertainty mitigation
  - coordination persistence safeguards
  - governance and monitoring recommendations

- `ethics_compliance`
  - Zero-PII enforcement
  - Temporal Moat compliance
  - Coordination > Identity semantics
  - auditability statements

## Production Readiness Flow

1. **Signal collection**: identity-free coordination signals are ingested through WhatsApp, API, or demo scripts.
2. **Coordination processing**: LUMOZA detects stable demand rhythms over 7-cycle windows and applies cross-validation with telemetry when available.
3. **Trust scoring**: ZENTARI assigns confidence and bankability notes to coordination patterns.
4. **Infrastructure analysis**: LUNDAI overlays settlement and infrastructure metadata to identify critical gaps and priority zones.
5. **Prospectus generation**: the `ProspectusGenerator` combines evidence into a deployment-ready package.

## What Production Ready Means for Kulima OS

A production-ready Kulima OS implementation must:

- produce reproducible, auditable guidance suitable for institutional review
- maintain privacy by disallowing individual-level data in outputs
- provide conservative, bankability-minded demand and infrastructure estimates
- surface regulatory and stakeholder engagement gaps explicitly
- include next-step actions for deployment and continued monitoring
- preserve essential service reserve capacity by design

## Current Production Readiness Implementation

The current repository implements production readiness through:

- `backend/api/` for API-first ingestion and interaction
- `core/lumoza/` for coordination signal intelligence
- `core/lundai/` for infrastructure gap and priority analysis
- `core/zentari/` for confidence and bankability scoring
- `core/prospectus/prospectus_generator.py` for deployment readiness and investor-facing artifacts

## Deployment Readiness Checklist

A production deployment should confirm:

- `POST /api/v1/signal` is available and validated
- `GET /api/v1/summary/{zone}` returns zone-level coordination summaries
- `POST /api/v1/generate-prospectus` produces a prospectus with `deployment_readiness`
- data persistence is configured for PostgreSQL in production
- WhatsApp webhook integration is secured and verified
- API authentication, rate limiting, and logging are enabled
- infrastructure planning outputs include reserve-aware capacity sizing
- stakeholder engagement and regulatory steps are documented in the prospectus
