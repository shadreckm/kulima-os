## FINAL ANSWER

### "If Palantir and Stripe had a child focused on agricultural decision intelligence in Africa, what would the product look like?"

**KULIMA OS 2.0:**

1. **Palantir's Decision Intelligence**
   - Data-driven recommendations, not dashboards
   - Evidence trails for every decision
   - Clear confidence indicators
   - Audit-ready provenance

2. **Stripe's Clarity & Simplicity**
   - Understand everything in 30 seconds
   - No unnecessary complexity
   - Mobile-first design
   - 3-tap workflows

3. **African Context**
   - Works offline
   - SMS/WhatsApp integration
   - Chichewa language support
   - Low-bandwidth optimized

4. **Trust-First Design**
   - Every recommendation answers "WHY?"
   - Visual confidence indicators
   - Evidence breakdown
   - No black boxes

**The Result:**

A decision intelligence platform that Grace Banda can open on her phone, understand where demand is highest within 30 seconds, see exactly why the system is confident, and approve a $120K resource allocation with full accountability to her donors.

**Not a dashboard. Not a survey tool. A decision engine.**

## KEY DESIGN DECISIONS

### 1. Priority Stack > Dashboard Grid
**Why:** Grace needs to know "what's most urgent" not "what's happening everywhere"

**Before:** 4x3 grid of widgets  
**After:** Vertical priority stack (red → yellow → green)

### 2. Demand Score > Technical Metrics
**Why:** Grace cares about "how much demand" not "LUMOZA output: 0.92"

**Before:** Coordination confidence: 0.92  
**After:** Demand Score: 92/100

### 3. Plain Language > Technical Terms
**Why:** Grace is not a data scientist

**Before:** ZENTARI trust evaluation  
**After:** How sure we are: 94%

### 4. Evidence First > Algorithm First
**Why:** Trust comes from seeing the evidence, not trusting the algorithm

**Before:** "AI detected pattern"  
**After:** "42 farmer requests + 7 dealer reports + 3 officer checks"

### 5. Action-Oriented > Information-Oriented
**Why:** Grace needs to decide, not just know

**Before:** "Seed shortage detected"  
**After:** "Deploy 600 bags within 7 days"
